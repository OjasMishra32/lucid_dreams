# THROWAWAY Quest test for Court Side, never merge

This is S6 v2, a headset benchmark page on branch `court-side-q0`. It covers:

- 1440p72 video streamed through WebCodecs, and the press that switches to a primed "ease" decoder.
- Spark sort lag, and whether hidden splats pop when revealed.
- VR vs AR-opaque, with an always-on edge fade in AR-opaque.
- What happens when the headset comes off.

You press one button; the phases run by themselves and end in a results table you photograph. The binary test assets
are generated by `make-assets.sh` and git ignores them. Nothing here touches the viewer.

## On the Mac (Ojasva)

From your `lucid_dreams` checkout:

```sh
git fetch origin
git worktree add ../q0 origin/court-side-q0
ln -s "$PWD/apps/webxr/node_modules" ../q0/apps/webxr/node_modules
../q0/apps/webxr/src/spike6/make-assets.sh
cd ../q0/apps/webxr && npm run dev
```

- `git worktree add` leaves your own checkout as it is.
- `make-assets.sh` needs ffmpeg and node. It takes about 2.5 min and writes `src/spike6/assets/`.
- If port 5180 is busy with your main checkout's server, stop that server, or run `npm run dev -- --port 5182` and
  point your tunnel at it.

Then open `https://<your address or tunnel>/court-quest.html` in the Quest browser.

To check it on the laptop first, open `http://localhost:<port>/court-quest.html?emulate&selftest&mode=both` with the
server started as `LUCID_HTTP=1 npm run dev -- --port 5182`. It runs both modes in the emulator, in about 2 min.

## In the headset

Use hands, not controllers; the palm menu opens when you turn your left palm up. One run in each mode, about 90 s each.

1. Set a **roomscale boundary of at least 3.5 x 3.5 m and keep it on** (Settings > Physical space > Boundary >
   Roomscale). The table shows the size it read, or "3.5 m square at start" if it got none.
2. Open the page. Allow spatial data if asked. Wait for "Loaded ... Pick a mode."
3. Press **Enter VR**. Stand where you entered, facing the court. The phases run by themselves; the panel on your left
   says what to do:
   - **4 Walk**: walk slowly around the two players, looking at them.
   - **8 and 9 Reveal**: keep watching the players. Do they pop in when the video disappears?
   - Every other phase: stand still. Don't take the headset off or open the Meta menu until RESULTS shows.
4. When **RESULTS** appears, photograph the left panel.
5. Take the headset off, **count to 10**, put it back on. The Off/on line on the left panel is now filled.
6. Answer on the **right panel**: aim a hand at a button (a thin ray shows where) and pinch.

   **Safety for these two:** walk slowly, a hand out in front of you, with a second person spotting you. Stop if no
   wall appears.
   - Walk to the edge of your boundary. Does the boundary wall or grid appear?
   - Only if it appeared: step one foot past it. Does your room show through? If no wall appeared, press **Skip**
     and don't step past.
7. Photograph both panels. Then palm menu > **Exit**. The tables are also on the page: screenshot them.
8. Press **Enter AR-opaque** and repeat steps 3-7. It looks like VR. There are three differences:
   - The last phase, "See my room", shows your room for 3 s.
   - The room also fades in as you near the edge: it starts about 0.6 m out and is fully visible at 0.3 m.
   - A third question tests that fade: walk slowly toward the edge of the play area and watch. The right panel shows
     your live distance to the edge.

   For the two safety questions above this fade is switched off (the panel says "fade OFF"), so that they test the
   headset's own boundary.
9. Send the photos, plus anything odd: a tab crash or reload, flicker, stutter in the video, the players popping in,
   or the room not showing.

If something goes wrong:

- If the page reloads, the last results come back at the top of the page.
- Palm menu > **Questions** re-asks the questions. Palm menu > **Restart** reruns the phases.
- If a "WC errors" or "SW" decoder line appears, add `?wc=1080` to the URL for the 1080p stream.

## Afterwards

From your `lucid_dreams` checkout: `git worktree remove --force ../q0`. That deletes the worktree and its generated
assets. The branch stays on GitHub until someone deletes it; it is never merged.
