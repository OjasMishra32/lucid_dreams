# Court Side headset checks

For the person in the headset, with the presenter beside you. Tick **yes** only when the line under "You should see" is true. A **no** gets a short note on the right: what you saw, and when. Pre-flight: run `node apps/webxr/scripts/court/qa/run.mjs` first; it must print `exit 0`.

- **Q1** = the stand-in on the headset. **Q2** = the real bundle. **Q3** = the full run (and ten times in a row, item 36).
- **Every session starts with the presenter's wall-walk (items 31 and 32), before anyone else wears the headset.** Then begin at item 1.
- Items are in the order the judge meets them. A round is only listed when it applies to that item.
- No numbers from the broadcast are on this sheet. Distances and times below are the experience's own design numbers.
