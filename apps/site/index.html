Lucid Dreams

Anyone from any video, standing in your room.

A photo or a video, and one sentence of what you want to do with them. They’re in your room, life-size, doing it with you.

How it works

48

views around them

4DAnyone turns one video into 48 consistent views. Three rings of 16 cameras that were never there.

4DAnyone, Ant Research. Public August 2026.

12

GPUs per person

Every frame is rebuilt as 3D Gaussians on UF’s HiPerGator, each GPU taking a slice of the clip.

B200RTX PRO 6000

1:1

on your floor

Life-size in passthrough on Quest 3S, or on a laptop. Walk around them. Never scaled.

WebXR, local-floor. Their feet on your floor.

Uses

Who would you
bring in?

    Real people, rebuilt this weekend from a video or a photo, and walked all the way around.

    1. Learn a move from a pro.

      A basketball jump shot, slowed down step by step, in your driveway.

      Teach me this jump shot.Teach

    2. A teacher in your room.

      A guitar strum or a dance step. Your turn after every step.

      Show me the strum, slowly.Teach

    3. Physical therapy at home.

      Your therapist’s exercise video becomes your therapist, life-size, checking your form.

      Walk me through my knee exercises.Teach

    4. Someone you’ve lost.

      An old home video of a grandparent. Their voice, and their words beside them. Consent first.

      I just want to sit with her.Companion

    5. Old film, brought back.

      Buster Keaton, 1920, public domain, standing in your living room.

      Let me see him up close.Companion

    6. You, perfected.

      Your own body doing a pro’s move: a rigged avatar from your photo, moving with their motion.

      Show me doing his jump shot.Teach

    7. Chess with the legends.

      Stockfish at Fischer’s and Carlsen’s peak ratings, across a real-size board. Hands only.

      Play chess with me.Play

    8. Captions for everyone.

      What they say floats beside them, where they stand. Their voice comes from there too.
