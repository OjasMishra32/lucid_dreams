02 · Cameras

48 cameras that were never there.

4DAnyone imagines the person from every side, in three rings of 16: at −10°, 15° and 35°.

Each screen plays the view that camera would have filmed.

All 48, in sync, for every frame of the clip.

03 · Reconstruction

Then every frame becomes 3D.

Rebuilt from all 48 views as 3D Gaussians: tiny, soft, coloured ellipsoids.

06 · In your room

Life-size.
On your real floor.

Meta Quest 3S passthrough, in the browser, through WebXR. Their feet on your floor, never scaled. Walk around them.

1:1

Real size, from the bundle’s own metres.

0

Controllers. Hands only, with a palm-up menu on your left hand.

Pinned

To your room with WebXR anchors. No accidental gestures.

Things you can hold.

Stills from the WebXR viewer on a laptop. In the headset, the dark is your room.

  • A real 7-iron.

    And a ball on a tee. Swing beside the golfer.

  • A basketball.

    Shoot it at a regulation hoop, rim at 3.05 m.

  • A guitar you strum.

    Neck in your left hand, strings under your right. Chords on your palm.

  • Chess pieces.

    Pick them up, capture into your hand, hit the clock.

07 · Intent

One sentence becomes a plan.

Gemini watches the clip, reads your sentence and writes experience.json. Every player runs it.

  1. TeachEach step slowed and repeated. Then your turn.
  2. CompanionThey’re simply with you. Voice and captions.
  3. PlayA real-size board between you.
