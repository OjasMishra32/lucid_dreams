## Essential shot list

1. Record the Quest passthrough at eye height. Lock exposure and avoid showing private rooms, bystanders or browser chrome.
2. For the lesson, take one wide stable room shot (person, moving ball, hoop) and one close readable step-card shot. Keep the person’s feet and the hoop in frame for the wide shot.
3. For the restoration moment, use matched timestamps and the same display scale in the left/right comparison. Caption it “Lanczos baseline” and “Real-ESRGAN restoration”; do not call it an improvement if identity, faces, hands or motion look worse.
4. For the family moment, use only the scope actually approved in the anonymous consent record. Capture no private source screen if permission covers the likeness but not public source footage.
5. Include one clear seen-versus-imagined toggle in the film. State that blue is generated detail, then return to the normal view.
6. Capture the chess beat after the three people moments. Keep the required Stockfish label visible for its entire shot.

## Edit rules and final checks

- Use simple cuts, one quiet music bed only where no original voice is needed, and captions for every spoken narrator line.
- Keep original voice separate from narration and never synthesize a public figure’s voice. Use the device or a team narrator for all explanatory speech.
- Before export, confirm each public-media label and credit against [credits.md](credits.md), each family/team use against [consent.md](consent.md), and each number against an actual report.
- Export a 16:9 1080p master and a clean 3-minute-or-shorter upload. Keep raw recordings and edit project assets outside this public repository.
