# Test clip capture guide

Owner: Atina · Goal: three usable tests handed to Ojasva by Friday, October 23, 10 PM ET.

Prioritize a sharply recorded person over an elaborate move. The first batch tests the pipeline; it is not a claim of final demo quality.

## Set up once

- Get the person's agreement to likeness capture, processing and the intended demo uses before recording. Log it in [consent.md](consent.md). If audio is retained, include that use explicitly; do not clone anyone's voice.
- Use the rear camera on a tripod or firm support. Keep it still throughout each take. Use the best native recording quality available: **4K preferred, at least 1080p, 60 fps preferred**.
- Prefer portrait **9:16**. Landscape is accepted by the existing pipeline's automatic person crop, but it still needs room around the entire body and movement. Do not pre-crop away hands, head or feet.
- Use bright, even light and a plain background. Avoid backlighting, harsh shadows, mirrors, people crossing the shot, loose objects and clothing that hides the hands.
- Frame one person from head to feet with margin for the whole move. Let them fill most of the image. Rehearse the widest reach and highest point before recording.
- Where the camera allows, lock focus and exposure on the person and use a fast shutter. Check a moving hand in a recorded frame; increase light or slow the movement if it smears. Prefer a crisp image over a dim, noisy one.

## Record three tests

These are proposed shots, not existing footage. A consenting teammate can perform more than one.

| Planned ID | Move | What to capture | Framing check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| test-01 | Basketball shooting form | One controlled shooting motion, then settle; a ball is optional | Raised hands and any jump stay inside frame; both feet remain visible |
| test-02 | Simple dance step | One slow side step each way with a small arm movement | Leave space on both sides; no full spins in the first test |
| test-03 | Boxing combination | Two controlled punches followed by a reset | Keep hands visible at full extension; avoid rapid motion blur |

Record **5–10 seconds per take**, including a brief still pose at the start and end. The source needs **at least 121 frames**. Preserve the original frame rate and resolution; do not send a screen recording, social-media download, slow-motion export or heavily compressed message attachment as the master.

## Accept or retake — inspect on the phone

Play the whole take, then pause during the fastest movement and inspect the face, fingers and feet at full size.

- [ ] One consenting person; no bystanders, private screens or identifying background details.
- [ ] Whole body and all movement remain in frame, including feet on the floor.
- [ ] Camera stays still; focus and exposure do not visibly pulse.
- [ ] Face, hands and clothing detail are sharp during movement; no obvious smear.
- [ ] Bright, even image; plain background; person is easy to separate from it.
- [ ] 5–10 seconds; at least 1080p; actual resolution and fps recorded.
- [ ] Original file retained; consent covers processing and intended use before automatic intake.

Retake visible blur, lost focus, missing feet or clipped hands before handing the file over. Write down any remaining limitation rather than marking it as clean. Visual review is a source-quality check, not a reconstruction fidelity measurement.

## Name and hand off

Use descriptive names without personal information, for example `test-01-jumper-take01.mp4`, `test-02-dance-take01.mov`, and `test-03-boxing-take01.mp4`. Keep the camera's actual file extension; renaming an extension does not convert a video.

1. Add actual filename, subject/consent ID, capture settings, duration and quality result to [clips.md](clips.md).
2. AirDrop or copy approved originals to **Ojasva's Mac** and place them in `~/Downloads/lucid-clips/`. A same-named folder on another Mac is not connected to the autopilot.
3. Confirm the files arrived, then record time and confirmation. Do not resubmit under new filenames just because processing is not immediate; ask Ojasva for pipeline status.
4. Keep originals in private storage outside the repo. Never commit clips, family photos or generated Gaussian files.

For the family moment, follow [consent.md](consent.md) first. Do not put an unapproved family asset into automatic intake. Public-figure footage is not needed for this first test batch; any later candidate needs a source record and demo-only handling, with no voice cloning or AI speaking as that person.
